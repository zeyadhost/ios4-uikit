The gallery covers stock public widget types, not a reimplementation of UIKit APIs. Abstract/base classes such as `UIControl` and `UIBarItem` have no standalone look; their subclasses are shown. Controller flows, international keyboard layouts, dynamic dates, edit gestures, iPad-specific system variants and animation timing are not exhaustive. The class inventory includes `UIStepper`, but it was not a public API until iOS 5 and is not shown as a public iOS 4 control. Native screenshots or device measurements were not used to establish pixel parity.
